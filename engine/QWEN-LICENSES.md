# Optional Qwen3-TTS

The `qwen-tts` 0.1.1 engine and the Qwen3-TTS 12Hz 1.7B CustomVoice model are
published by Qwen under Apache-2.0.

- Code: https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS
- Model: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice/tree/0c0e3051f131929182e2c023b9537f8b1c68adfe
- Official license text: [QWEN-MODEL-LICENSE.txt](QWEN-MODEL-LICENSE.txt)

The model revision and SHA-256 fingerprints are in `qwen-tts-model.json`.
The download starts only from the preparation button in the settings. A copy
of the license and the revision reference are saved next to the model. The
Python dependencies keep their own licenses in their private environment; they
are not included in the documents or transcription environments.

The nine voices are the model's built-in multilingual voices. Qwen's Serena
voice is distinct from Piper's Italian Serena voice.
